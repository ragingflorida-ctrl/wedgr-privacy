WEDGR Privacy Policy

App: WEDGR (com.wedgr.app)
Effective date: September 25, 2026

WEDGR is a barcode, QR code and text scanner that sends what you scan to your own computer over Bluetooth. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and what leaves your device.

Summary

  • WEDGR has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics of its own.
  • Your scans, scan history and list of paired computers are stored only on your device.
  • Camera images are processed on your device and are never uploaded.
  • WEDGR uses Google ML Kit for on-device scanning. ML Kit sends Google limited technical information about the device and app, described below.

Information stored on your device

InformationPurpose

Names and Bluetooth addresses of up to five paired computers, with the date last connectedReconnecting to and switching between your computers

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings.

Information sent to Google by ML Kit

WEDGR uses Google ML Kit to recognise barcodes and text. Recognition runs entirely on your device, and images and scanned content are not sent to Google. To maintain and improve ML Kit, Google's ML Kit SDK sends Google the following technical information:

  • Device information (manufacturer, model, Android version)
  • App information (package name and app version)
  • A device or installation identifier that is not used to identify you personally
  • Performance metrics, API configuration, feature versions, event types and error codes

This information is encrypted in transit and, according to Google, is not transferred to third parties. See Google's disclosure: ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

What is sent to your computer

When the Bluetooth keyboard connection is active, each scanned value is typed into the computer you paired, exactly as a keyboard would. It goes directly from your phone to your computer over Bluetooth and does not pass through any server. Like any keyboard, WEDGR receives the computer's Caps Lock and Num Lock status so it can type correctly; this is used only while connected and is not stored.

Actions you choose

If you choose Open in Browser, Call, Send Email, Search Google, Share or Export CSV, the selected scan or file is passed to the app you pick (for example your browser, phone app, email app or a sharing target). That app handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Clear your scan history: Settings → Clear All Data, or Clear in the History Log.
  • Remove a paired computer: Settings → Manage Devices → Forget.
  • Remove everything: uninstall WEDGR, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → WEDGR.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing further to delete on our side.

Children

WEDGR is a productivity tool for business and professional use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
